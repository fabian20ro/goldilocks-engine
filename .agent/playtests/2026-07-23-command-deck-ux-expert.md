## Findings

### UX-PT-001 — Early safe batching dominates management

Severity: P1.

After Interactive Chat proved safe, queueing ten at 64x produced the first
purchase with no intervening decision. The visible Long Document alternative
was effectively guaranteed failure, teaching avoidance rather than a
recoverable tradeoff.

Product implication: before the first meaningful module purchase, expose at
least two forecastable and materially viable operational routes. Fast batching
may remain convenient but must not eliminate all early management decisions.

### UX-PT-002 — Placement state leaks beyond its interaction context

Severity: P1.

After selecting/equipping Precision Cleaner, selected state, Cancel, Snap here
targets, and the Build pending badge remained while browsing Career and Inspect.

Product implication: separate item inspection from placement. Preserve only an
explicit Upgrades-to-Build handoff; unrelated navigation clears placement state.

### UX-PT-003 — Onboarding explains more than it directs

Severity: P1.

The initial view presents accurate but dense rig, warning, objective, pipeline,
module, and quick-start information before the first job. The actionable first
path is Jobs, Interactive Chat, Queue 1.

Product implication: stage the first session around queue, settle, then
buy/install. Retain complete explanation through Help and item Details.

### UX-PT-004 — Precision competes with initial scanability

Severity: P2.

Compute units, reserve, modeled delivery, evidence, debt, and exact accounting
appear early and often.

Product implication: summaries lead with one rounded decision metric and
outcome language; exact live values remain in Details, Inspect, and settlement
evidence.

### UX-PT-005 — Settlement is trustworthy but emotionally restrained

Severity: P2.

Success and failure are legible but primarily text-led. The first purchase
creates more satisfaction than repeated successful work.

Product implication: add restrained, reduced-motion-safe settlement, milestone,
and next-affordable-target feedback without casino presentation or hidden
rewards.

## Scope classification

- Usability: onboarding action hierarchy, cross-tab placement state, default
  information density.
- Early-loop game design: safe batching dominance and insufficient viable
  short-horizon forks.
- Presentation/taste: restrained feedback; preserve the niche terminal tone.
- Missing-content/retention: long-term variety was not measured. Treat later
  contract, synergy, Career, and Research hooks as hypotheses requiring their
  own deterministic and playtest evidence.
